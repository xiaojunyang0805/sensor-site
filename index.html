About SeeNano

A deep-tech company making sensitive electrochemical sensing accessible

SeeNano Technology B.V. was founded in November 2021 in Enschede, the Netherlands. We work at the meeting point of nanotechnology and electrochemistry, developing instruments and electrodes that detect extremely small signals — down to single particles and single molecules.

Our mission is to democratise single-molecule sensing: to take capability that today exists mainly inside well-funded laboratories and package it into affordable, portable platforms that any research group or product team can pick up and use.

We work with universities, R&D laboratories, startups and product developers across health, environmental monitoring and wearable sensing — supplying hardware, developing application-specific electrodes, and collaborating on applied research.

The gap we close

Sensitive electrochemical sensing is well proven in the literature — and largely out of reach in practice.

💸

Instruments cost too much

Research-grade potentiostats run to four figures, which puts sensitive measurement out of reach for smaller labs, startups and teaching use.

📉

Electrode quality limits results

Inconsistent electrodes produce unreliable signals, so good instruments still give results that are hard to trust or reproduce.

🔬

Proven, but not productised

Single-entity and single-molecule sensing work in published research, yet almost none of it has been turned into something you can buy and use.

We close that gap from both sides at once — an affordable instrument paired with electrodes engineered for the measurement you actually need to make.
